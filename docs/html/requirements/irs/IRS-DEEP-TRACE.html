    • 1. Patient monitor interface
    • SSS005
      1.5. Attach selects the default profile SSS005

      attach shall store the patient and select that patient's default limit profile.
      • SRS001
        1.1. Present vital signs SRS001

        When monitoring is running and the sensor is valid, the monitor shall present the attached patient's heart rate in beats per minute, oxygen saturation in percent, systolic and diastolic blood pressure in millimetres of mercury, and temperature in degrees Celsius.
    IRS001
    1.1. attach IRS001

    attach shall accept a Human and shall select the default limit profile for that patient's age.
    • SSS004
      1.4. Idle and stopped monitor SSS004

      Before start and after stop, the monitor shall report no alarms and shall not present vitals as valid. start with no patient attached shall be rejected.
      • SRS001
        1.1. Present vital signs SRS001

        When monitoring is running and the sensor is valid, the monitor shall present the attached patient's heart rate in beats per minute, oxygen saturation in percent, systolic and diastolic blood pressure in millimetres of mercury, and temperature in degrees Celsius.
    IRS002
    1.2. start and stop IRS002

    start shall begin monitoring when a patient is attached and shall reject a start when no patient is attached. stop shall end monitoring.
    • SSS007
      1.7. Reject out-of-range limit command SSS007

      set_limits shall reject a command whose heart-rate low limit is outside 20 through 200, whose heart-rate high limit is outside 30 through 250 or not strictly above the low limit, whose SpO2 low limit is outside 50 through 100, whose systolic high limit is outside 80 through 250, whose diastolic high limit is outside 40 through 150, or whose temperature limits are outside 30.0 through 43.0 or whose temperature high limit is not strictly above the low limit. A rejected command shall not be used for alarm evaluation, and the previously accepted limits shall remain.
      • SRS004
        1.4. Reject an out-of-range limit command SRS004

        The monitor shall reject an alarm-limit command outside the allowed range and shall leave the limits already in effect unchanged.
    IRS003
    1.3. set_limits IRS003

    set_limits shall accept an in-range AlarmLimits and shall raise LimitRejected without changing the limits already in effect when the command is out of range.
    • SSS001
      1.1. Report five vital values SSS001

      While monitoring is running and the sensor is valid, the monitor shall report the attached patient's heart rate, oxygen saturation, systolic pressure, diastolic pressure, and temperature unchanged.
      • SRS001
        1.1. Present vital signs SRS001

        When monitoring is running and the sensor is valid, the monitor shall present the attached patient's heart rate in beats per minute, oxygen saturation in percent, systolic and diastolic blood pressure in millimetres of mercury, and temperature in degrees Celsius.
    • SSS006
      1.6. Sensor fault risk control SSS006

      When the sensor is invalid, the monitor shall raise only SENSOR_FAULT and shall not present vitals as valid.
      • SRS003
        1.3. Sensor-fault alarm SRS003

        The monitor shall raise a sensor-fault alarm when the measurement is invalid, and shall not raise a vital-sign alarm from an invalid measurement.
    IRS004
    1.4. vitals and alarms IRS004

    vitals shall return a reading whose valid flag is true only while monitoring is running with a valid sensor. alarms shall return the active alarm set and shall return an empty set when monitoring is not running.
    • SSS010

      SoapPatientDevice shall return a Human matching the GetPatient body, including sensor_valid false when the body says the sensor is invalid.
      • SRS006
        1.6. Obtain a patient over SOAP SRS006

        The SOAP patient device shall obtain identity, age, vitals, and sensor validity from a SOAP 1.1 GetPatient response.
    • SSS011
      3.2. SOAP fault does not return a patient SSS011

      A SOAP fault or an unknown patient shall not produce a Human.
      • SRS006
        1.6. Obtain a patient over SOAP SRS006

        The SOAP patient device shall obtain identity, age, vitals, and sensor validity from a SOAP 1.1 GetPatient response.
    IRS005
    1.5. SOAP GetPatient IRS005

    GetPatient shall accept a patientId and shall return that patient's identity, age, vitals, and sensor validity, or a SOAP fault when the patient is unknown or the request is malformed.
