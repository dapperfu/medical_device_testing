      1.2. Select the limit profile by age SSS002

      Age 18 years or older shall select the adult profile, and age under 18 years shall select the pediatric profile. Adult alarms are heart rate below 50 or above 120 bpm, SpO2 below 90 percent, systolic pressure above 160 mmHg, diastolic pressure above 100 mmHg, and temperature below 35.0 or above 38.0 degrees Celsius. Pediatric alarms are heart rate below 70 or above 160 bpm, SpO2 below 92 percent, systolic pressure above 120 mmHg, diastolic pressure above 80 mmHg, and temperature below 35.5 or above 38.0 degrees Celsius.

        The monitor shall raise an alarm when a valid vital sign is outside the limit profile for that patient's age.

        1.1. Present vital signs SRS001

    SDD001
    1.1. Human stimulus SDD001

    Human and its patient subclasses shall hold the patient stimulus, including age and sensor validity, and shall not evaluate alarms.
    • SSS007
      1.7. Reject out-of-range limit command SSS007

      set_limits shall reject a command whose heart-rate low limit is outside 20 through 200, whose heart-rate high limit is outside 30 through 250 or not strictly above the low limit, whose SpO2 low limit is outside 50 through 100, whose systolic high limit is outside 80 through 250, whose diastolic high limit is outside 40 through 150, or whose temperature limits are outside 30.0 through 43.0 or whose temperature high limit is not strictly above the low limit. A rejected command shall not be used for alarm evaluation, and the previously accepted limits shall remain.
      • SRS004
        1.4. Reject an out-of-range limit command SRS004

        The monitor shall reject an alarm-limit command outside the allowed range and shall leave the limits already in effect unchanged.
