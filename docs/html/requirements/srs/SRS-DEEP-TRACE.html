    SRS001
    1.1. Present vital signs SRS001

    When monitoring is running and the sensor is valid, the monitor shall present the attached patient's heart rate in beats per minute, oxygen saturation in percent, systolic and diastolic blood pressure in millimetres of mercury, and temperature in degrees Celsius.
    • SSS001
      1.1. Report five vital values SSS001

      While monitoring is running and the sensor is valid, the monitor shall report the attached patient's heart rate, oxygen saturation, systolic pressure, diastolic pressure, and temperature unchanged.
      • IRS004
        1.4. vitals and alarms IRS004

        vitals shall return a reading whose valid flag is true only while monitoring is running with a valid sensor. alarms shall return the active alarm set and shall return an empty set when monitoring is not running.
      • SDD004
        1.4. PatientMonitor unit SDD004

        PatientMonitor shall own attach, start, stop, set_limits, vitals, and alarms, and shall keep the last accepted limits when a command is rejected.
      • TD011
        2.1. Healthy adult integration TD011

        test_healthy_adult_reports_vitals_and_no_alarm shall present the healthy adult vitals unchanged, with the adult profile and no alarm.
      • TD019
        3.1. System vitals TD019

        test_system_reports_attached_vitals shall present the five healthy-adult vital values while monitoring is running.
      • TP002
        1.2. Integration with each patient TP002

        Integration tests in tests/integration shall combine PatientMonitor with each patient fixture.
    • SSS004
      1.4. Idle and stopped monitor SSS004

      Before start and after stop, the monitor shall report no alarms and shall not present vitals as valid. start with no patient attached shall be rejected.
      • IRS002
        1.2. start and stop IRS002

        start shall begin monitoring when a patient is attached and shall reject a start when no patient is attached. stop shall end monitoring.
      • SDD004
        1.4. PatientMonitor unit SDD004

        PatientMonitor shall own attach, start, stop, set_limits, vitals, and alarms, and shall keep the last accepted limits when a command is rejected.
      • TD018
        2.8. Idle and stopped integration TD018

        test_idle_and_stopped_monitor_has_no_alarms shall report no alarms before start and after stop, and shall alarm only while running.
      • TD022
        3.4. System start and stop TD022

        test_system_start_and_stop shall reject start with no patient and shall clear alarms after stop.
    • SSS005
      1.5. Attach selects the default profile SSS005

      attach shall store the patient and select that patient's default limit profile.
      • IRS001
        1.1. attach IRS001

        attach shall accept a Human and shall select the default limit profile for that patient's age.
      • SDD001

      • SDD004
        1.4. PatientMonitor unit SDD004

    SRS002
    1.2. Alarm on out-of-limit vitals SRS002

    The monitor shall raise an alarm when a valid vital sign is outside the limit profile for that patient's age.
    • SSS002
      1.2. Select the limit profile by age SSS002

      Age 18 years or older shall select the adult profile, and age under 18 years shall select the pediatric profile. Adult alarms are heart rate below 50 or above 120 bpm, SpO2 below 90 percent, systolic pressure above 160 mmHg, diastolic pressure above 100 mmHg, and temperature below 35.0 or above 38.0 degrees Celsius. Pediatric alarms are heart rate below 70 or above 160 bpm, SpO2 below 92 percent, systolic pressure above 120 mmHg, diastolic pressure above 80 mmHg, and temperature below 35.5 or above 38.0 degrees Celsius.
      • SDD001
        1.1. Human stimulus SDD001

        Human and its patient subclasses shall hold the patient stimulus, including age and sensor validity, and shall not evaluate alarms.
      • SDD003
        1.3. AlarmEvaluator unit SDD003

        AlarmEvaluator shall be a pure function from a patient and a limit profile to a set of alarms, including the inclusive-limit and sensor-fault rules.
      • TD001
        1.1. Age selects population TD001

        test_age_selects_population shall show that age 18 is not pediatric and age 17 is pediatric.
      • TD002
        1.2. Patients inherit from Human TD002

      • TD003
        1.3. Default profiles accepted TD003

        test_default_profiles_are_accepted shall accept the adult and pediatric defaults and shall map age 18 to adult and age 17 to pediatric.
      • TD008
        1.8. One past the limit TD008

      • TD009
        1.9. Pediatric limits differ TD009

      • TD011
        2.1. Healthy adult integration TD011

      • TD012
        2.2. Tachycardic adult integration TD012

      • TD013
        2.3. Hypoxic adult integration TD013

      • TD014
        2.4. Hypertensive adult integration TD014

        test_hypertensive_adult_blood_pressure_alarm shall raise only BLOOD_PRESSURE for the hypertensive adult.
      • TD015
        2.5. Pediatric integration TD015

        test_pediatric_patient_uses_pediatric_profile shall select the pediatric profile and shall not alarm at heart rate 140.
      • TD020
        3.2. System profile by age TD020

    • SSS003
      1.3. Inclusive limits SSS003

      A vital sign equal to its limit shall not raise that vital's alarm.
      • SDD003
        1.3. AlarmEvaluator unit SDD003

      • TD007

    SRS004
    1.4. Reject an out-of-range limit command SRS004

    The monitor shall reject an alarm-limit command outside the allowed range and shall leave the limits already in effect unchanged.
    • SSS007
      1.7. Reject out-of-range limit command SSS007

      set_limits shall reject a command whose heart-rate low limit is outside 20 through 200, whose heart-rate high limit is outside 30 through 250 or not strictly above the low limit, whose SpO2 low limit is outside 50 through 100, whose systolic high limit is outside 80 through 250, whose diastolic high limit is outside 40 through 150, or whose temperature limits are outside 30.0 through 43.0 or whose temperature high limit is not strictly above the low limit. A rejected command shall not be used for alarm evaluation, and the previously accepted limits shall remain.
      • IRS003
        1.3. set_limits IRS003

        set_limits shall accept an in-range AlarmLimits and shall raise LimitRejected without changing the limits already in effect when the command is out of range.
      • SDD002
        1.2. AlarmLimits unit SDD002

        AlarmLimits shall hold one limit profile and accept_limits shall decide whether a set_limits command is inside the allowed range.
      • SDD004
        1.4. PatientMonitor unit SDD004

        PatientMonitor shall own attach, start, stop, set_limits, vitals, and alarms, and shall keep the last accepted limits when a command is rejected.
      • TD003
        1.3. Default profiles accepted TD003

        test_default_profiles_are_accepted shall accept the adult and pediatric defaults and shall map age 18 to adult and age 17 to pediatric.
      • TD004
        1.4. Reject SpO2 above 100 TD004

        test_rejects_spo2_above_100 shall reject an SpO2 low limit of 101.
      • TD005
        1.5. Reject heart-rate order TD005

        test_rejects_heart_rate_high_not_above_low shall reject a heart-rate high limit that is not above the low limit.
      • TD006
        1.6. Reject temperature order TD006

        test_rejects_temperature_high_not_above_low shall reject a temperature high limit that is not above the low limit.
      • TD017
        2.7. Rejected limits integration TD017

        test_rejected_limits_leave_the_adult_profile shall keep the adult profile after a rejected command and shall not alarm the healthy adult.
      • TD025
        3.7. System rejected command TD025

        test_system_rejects_out_of_range_command shall keep the previous limits and shall not alarm the healthy adult after a rejected command.
      • TP001
        1.1. Unit tests before integration TP001

        Unit tests in tests/unit shall be executed before integration tests. They shall cover limit acceptance, inclusive boundaries, profile selection, and sensor fault.
      • TP003
        1.3. System coverage TP003

        System tests in tests/system shall cover every SSS requirement, including the risk controls SSS006 and SSS007.
